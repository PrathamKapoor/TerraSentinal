# TerraSentinel Research: Comprehensive Prior-Art Evaluation Matrix

This matrix establishes a thorough comparative audit between the state of the art in literature and open-source software, and the advances contributed by **TerraSentinel** (*Evidence-Grounded Satellite Intelligence for Flood Disaster Response*).

Every operational capability is audited across 7 rigorous dimensions:
1. **Capability Domain**
2. **What Already Exists in Prior Art?**
3. **Which Repositories Implement It?**
4. **Which Papers Study It?**
5. **What is Mature in Existing Solutions?**
6. **What Remains Weak or Unsolved in Existing Solutions?**
7. **What TerraSentinel Will Reuse vs. What TerraSentinel Contributes?**
